# Study workflow

The authoritative workflow is [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md). The exhaustive research passes and acceptance criteria are in [research-standard.md](research-standard.md).

Pinakes’s original sequence is retained: establish Greek and name identities; translate and audit; describe scenes/speeches/days; analyze lexical tokens; check homographs; write shared articles and scoped notes; integrate, register quotes, and verify. GreekIndexBounty adds source/witness evidence, a candidate inventory, line/category review, a full agent roster, honest usage accounting, and a trusted PR policy gate.

Do not reuse claims that Pinakes’s books are already present here. Do not rely on absolute local paths or private sibling repositories. Everything necessary to reproduce a contribution must be cited publicly or described in its source-access record. Follow `AGENTS.md` for agent-specific constraints.
