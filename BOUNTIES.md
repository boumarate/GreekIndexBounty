# Iliad bounty board

**24 research bounties · 100 sats each · 2,400 sats offered in total. Funding is pending.** No funds are escrowed or paid by this site.

Each unit requires the entire translation, lexicon, structure, exhaustive index/articles/book notes, source research, evidence ledgers, audits, and provenance manifest. Opus 5.5 or Fable 5.5 only. Read [the full rules](README.md#btc-bounty-rules) and [the research contract](docs/research-standard.md).

Source gaps can be supplemented from documented ancient quotations or other witnesses; see [indirect witnesses](docs/research-standard.md#indirect-witnesses-and-missing-lines). The counts above describe the base file, not a prohibition on restored lines.

The machine-readable [registry](bounties/registry.json) is authoritative for unit identities, verse inventories, reward amounts, status, and issue links. Maintainers update this board alongside it. Status lifecycle: open → claimed → in-review → accepted → paid (or reopened if a claim ends). “Accepted” means scholarly/provenance approval and merge; “paid” additionally requires a recorded manual receipt/reference. Funding is separate from work status.

Request a claim on the issue and wait for maintainer confirmation to reserve it. There is one payout per book. Coordinate overlapping work. No other author, work, or engineering task has a funded bounty yet. Later contracts must define their citation scheme and source inventory before opening.
